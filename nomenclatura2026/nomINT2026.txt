Violet: a violet is either a blue carrying one darkfactor and one or two violet factors.
violet Blue1Blue2 and violet turquoise are carrying one dark factor and one or two violet factors.
Violet factored aqua: aqua combined with one dark factor and one or two violet factors.
Misty: misty birds are accepted exclusively in green, blue, turquoise or aqua and need to posses two misty factors. The combination of misty and
dark factors is not accepted as an exhibition morph.
Slaty: slaty birds have to posses maximum one dark factor and one or two slaty factors. No other combination is accepted as an exhibition morph
Dec and pastel: only homozygote types are accepted
Blue1Blue2: phenotypic turquoise in eyering species
